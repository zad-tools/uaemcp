# Open Emirates Intelligence

## Register

product

## Users

Developers connecting AI agents, researchers validating official UAE evidence, journalists and analysts checking public claims, and founders building UAE-focused applications. They need fast discovery without sacrificing provenance or understanding of data limits.

## Product Purpose

Make official UAE public data queryable through MCP, REST and focused bilingual applications. Success means a user can find a source, understand what it measures, inspect the exact evidence and limitations, and reuse the result without the platform inventing missing coverage.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, civic, exact. The product should feel like a modern public-data institution with the confidence of an editorial research desk. Its voice is direct, bilingual and honest about uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, glossy government portals, glassmorphism, neon AI dashboards, rounded card grids, decorative charts, unqualified national claims, and interfaces that hide methodology behind marketing language.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before narrative: show source, scope and limitations beside every claim.
2. Public infrastructure, not a demo: every visual surface must expose a reusable API or MCP contract.
3. Arabic is native: RTL, terminology and runtime states receive the same care as English.
4. Density earns trust: use structured ledgers and source-native records instead of decorative summaries.
5. Unknown stays unknown: never turn missing, inconsistent or bounded evidence into certainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All controls require accessible names, keyboard operation and visible focus. Dynamic results use live regions. Color is never the only status signal. Respect reduced motion and preserve complete functionality on mobile in both LTR and RTL.
