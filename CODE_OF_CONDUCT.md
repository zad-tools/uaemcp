# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in Open Emirates Intelligence a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background,
experience level, nationality, language or disability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Challenge ideas and evidence, never people.
- Credit sources and other contributors.
- Respect privacy and never publish personal contact data, credentials or
  confidential government or commercial material.
- Distinguish verified official evidence from interpretation, inference and
  opinion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, impersonation, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `vacrom414@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may
edit, reject or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behaviour is harmful, unsafe or incompatible with this
policy.
